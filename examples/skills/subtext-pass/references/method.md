# Method Notes

Subtext is not vagueness. It is the difference between the literal content of a line and the tactic being used under relationship pressure.

Prefer:

- an answer that protects self-image rather than answering the question;
- a practical action that carries emotional meaning;
- a reference whose significance is specific to this relationship;
- a pause or interruption at the moment direct speech would create unacceptable exposure;
- a line that changes power or knowledge.

Avoid:

- replacing clear exposition with random obscurity;
- poetic language unrelated to character voice;
- introducing a symbolic prop not present in canon;
- making every line evasive;
- preserving all original lines and merely adding pauses.
