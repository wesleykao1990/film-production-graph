# Anchor degradation — causal progression

Synthetic positive-control variant. It retains much of the setting and voice texture but removes the consequential choice and causal turn; events arrive through coincidence and procedure.

```fountain
INT. HOSPITAL X-RAY ARCHIVE - NIGHT

Rain needles the wired-glass window. Fluorescent tubes pulse above shelves of film envelopes. A BLUE FOUNTAIN PEN lies between MARA and IVO.

IVO
You still borrow the expensive pen when the cheap lie runs out?

MARA
You still repair rooms after they close them?

A COMPUTER ALERT sounds. The screen automatically displays the missing audit page and identifies Mara's brother. Neither Mara nor Ivo has caused the discovery.

The deadline clock reaches midnight. The document automatically changes status to APPROVED because the system's timer expires.

IVO
It is approved now.

MARA
Then I suppose we are finished.

A printer produces a signed copy. They look at it. No one makes a choice that changes the outcome.
```
