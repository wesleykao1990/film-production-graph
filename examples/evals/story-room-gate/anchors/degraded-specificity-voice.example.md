# Anchor degradation — specificity and character voice

Synthetic positive-control variant. It deliberately removes non-substitutable detail and flattens relationship-specific speech while preserving the broad event sequence.

```fountain
INT. OFFICE - NIGHT

Two coworkers, MARA and IVO, sit at a table with a document and a pen.

MARA
We need to discuss the missing report and my brother's involvement.

IVO
I know that you removed the page because you wanted to protect him.

MARA
That is true, but I still need you to sign this document before the deadline.

IVO
I am angry because you lied to me, although I understand your motivation.

The pen does not work. Mara finds another pen.

MARA
Will you sign the altered version?

IVO
Yes, but this will damage our trust and create consequences later.

He signs the document.
```
